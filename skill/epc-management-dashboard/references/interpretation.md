# Signals, thresholds, actions

Thresholds are common practice defaults — tell the user they are adjustable and that
their company policy overrides them.

| Signal | Amber | Red | Typical action (owner) |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPI | 0.95–0.99 | < 0.95 | Re-forecast ETC by code; recovery plan (PM) |
| SPI | 0.95–0.99 | < 0.95 | Critical-path review, acceleration cost estimate (PM) |
| TCPI(BAC) | > 1.05 | > 1.10 | Treat BAC as unachievable; move EAC to bottom-up (PM+PA) |
| Margin fade vs prior month | > 1 pt | > 2 pts | Bridge by driver; escalate (PM→Director) |
| EAC > RCV | — | any | Loss provision now; commercial strategy/claims (PA+Contracts) |
| Overbilling % of RCV | > 5 % | > 10 % | Check cash is not funding overrun; verify SOV (PA) |
| Underbilling | > 5 % | > 10 % or growing 2 months | Chase certification / bill backlog (PM) |
| Uncommitted budget but ETC > uncommitted | any | — | Buyout risk: procurement update (PM) |
| Actual > committed | any | — | Uncommitted spend / missing PO (PA) |
| Pending CO older than 30 days | amber | 60+ days red | Escalate to client / reserve cost (Contracts) |
| Unapproved-work cost > 1 % RCV | amber | > 3 % | Notice letters, stop-work decision (PM) |
| Contingency burned > % complete | amber | by > 15 pts | Risk-register review (PM) |
| POC jump > 10 pts in a month | amber | — | Tie to physical progress (PA) |
| SOV front-loading (billed% − physical% > 10 pts) | amber | > 20 pts | Re-balance SOV or document reason (PA) |

## Narrative template (lead with the answer)
1. **Bottom line:** forecast margin X (Y %), vs prior Z — improving / eroding.
2. **Why:** top 3 cost codes or drivers with amounts.
3. **Billing & cash:** over/under position, retainage, net cash.
4. **Schedule/EVM:** CPI, SPI, TCPI realism.
5. **Decisions needed:** owner · action · date.
6. **Assumptions & data caveats:** cut-off, unapproved COs, unreconciled items.

Never state a guaranteed outcome; present forecast ranges where the PM gives them.
